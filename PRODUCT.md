# Product context

## Register
product

## Platform
Web

## Purpose
A tablet-first eyewear point-of-sale for Eluno store associates, shared with customers during the purchase. Support safe customer lookup, prescription validation, quoting, payment, full refunds and day-end reporting against the existing local POS API.

## Users and setting
Store associates work at a bright counter using a touch tablet, often while a customer looks on. They need legible totals, precise clinical information, fast selection and trustworthy payment recovery.

## Principles
- Make the next action and amount due unmistakable.
- Preserve Eluno's identity without turning a working tool into a marketing page.
- Never hide pending or uncertain payment states.
- Use the backend as the authority for clinical validation, prices and taxes.
- Design for landscape and portrait tablet sizes and keyboard accessibility.
